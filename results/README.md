# Results from Hazel

Only the Zr6Ga2Ni benchmark's results may be committed here (`results/zr6ga2ni/`). Results for the candidate
compounds stay private until NC State's Office of Research Commercialization has reviewed them: the
owner's decision of 2026-11-19, recorded in `AGENTS.md` and `docs/hazel-candidates.md`. Everything else
under `results/` is ignored by git, and candidate results are kept under `data/raw/hazel/`.
